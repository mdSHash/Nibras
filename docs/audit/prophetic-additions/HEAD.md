  Every bio and every date read in full.

  Muslim, the brotherhood, events witnessed) and 45 corrections, 10 people checked with no finding. Sources: Usd al-Ghaba
  for 71 people, al-Istiʿab for 2, Ibn Hisham for the rest and for cross-checks. Birth and death dates left to 4E (conflicts

  events get a new timeline position from the Hijri date their sources give; 12 get a month added to the shown date.
  **One order change:** al-Israʾ moves after the first ʿAqaba if its shown date «قبل الهجرة بسنة» is kept (D-09). No event
  falls in another era; the Medinan era still starts with the Hijra, after the second ʿAqaba.
- **New records (03): 5 events and 3 companions** (N-01 to N-08 on the review page: N-01 the raid of Waddan, N-02 the brotherhood, N-03 Hamraʾ al-Asad, N-04 the letters to the kings, N-05 Abu Bakr's Hajj and Baraʾa, N-06 Usayd ibn Hudayr, N-07 ʿUthman ibn Mazʿun, N-08 Abu Salama), every fact with its page; plus L-01, linking ʿUrwa ibn
  Masʿud to al-Hudaybiyya. 294 source quotes in all, each found on its page by script. The new records pass the content
  schema (`shared/contentSchema.ts`), and every person they name exists.
- **The worst errors in the bios:**
  - **Nine bios talk about the app instead of the person** (al-Husayn, Abu Sufyan, Jabir, Fatima al-Zahraʾ, Duʿthur,
    ʿAbd Allah ibn Ubayy, ʿAmir ibn al-Tufayl, al-Ansar, Jibril: «يرد ذكره في التطبيق…», «ضمن النطاق التاريخي للمشروع»).
  - **Jabir ibn ʿAbd Allah** «شهد المشاهد بعد بدر»: he says himself he missed Badr *and* Uhud (B-085, Usd al-Ghaba).
  - **Sawda's first husband** died in Mecca after returning from Abyssinia, not in Abyssinia (Ibn Hisham).
  - **Khubayb** «حبسوه عند صفوان بن أمية»: Safwan bought Zayd ibn al-Dathinna; Khubayb was bought for ʿUqba ibn al-Harith.
  - **Al-Baraʾ ibn Maʿrur** «أول من صلى إلى الكعبة»: Kaʿb ibn Malik reports the Prophet ﷺ told him to keep the Jerusalem
    qibla, and he did.
  - **Rayhana** «فأعتقها»: Ibn Ishaq has her choosing to stay in his ownership rather than marry.
  - **Nusayba's Uhud passage** rests on Ibn Saʿd (cited in the bio, not on the reliable list); Ibn Hisham has her own account.
  - **Asʿad ibn Zurara:** al-Waqidi's story told without attribution, and a saying of the Prophet ﷺ found in no source.
- **The dates:** most Medinan events sat on 1 January of their Gregorian year: Badr (17 Ramadan 2 AH = 13 March 624) sat at
  624.0, before the qibla change and Nakhla; Uhud at 625.0; al-Hudaybiyya at 628.0; the Prophet's death at 632.3 (April).

## Decisions for Mostafa

1. **Approve, change or reject each entry.** Proven and likely ones are ready to apply; the three uncertain dates need your call.
2. **Al-Israʾ (D-09):** keep the shown date «قبل الهجرة بسنة» and move it after the first ʿAqaba (recommended), or keep it
   before ʿAqaba and change the shown date to «قبل الهجرة بستة عشر شهرًا (وقيل غير ذلك)». Al-Raheeq does not choose.
3. **Banu al-Mustaliq (D-26):** Ibn Ishaq and al-Raheeq say Shaʿban 6 AH; the app has 5 AH (the editor of Ibn Hisham argues
   for 5). Recommended: keep 5 AH and add «(وقيل 6 هـ)» to the shown date.
4. **Nusayba (B-056):** rewrite her Uhud passage from Ibn Hisham (recommended), or keep it with «ويروى».
5. **New records:** which of the 5 events and 3 companions to add. **Each new event is 2 recordings.**
6. **Pre-Hijra dates** are converted with the tabular calendar counted back in lunar years (year N of the Prophethood =
   Hijri year N − 13, as al-Raheeq counts it, p. 148). It is an approximation (the Arabs intercalated months before Islam);
   only the order and the month matter on the timeline.
