- **The worst problems:**
  - **Hadiths and sayings given to the wrong person or occasion.** «كلمة حق يراد بها باطل» is placed at the raising of
    the mushafs, but ʿAli said it of the Khawarij's «لا حكم إلا لله» (Muslim 1066). «فزت ورب الكعبة» is given to ʿAli; in
    al-Bukhari (4092) it is Haram ibn Milhan's. «يوسف هذه الأمة» is given to the Prophet ﷺ; al-Istiʿab and Usd al-Ghaba
    give it to ʿUmar. «فارقتني شيخا وجئتني أمرد» is given to ʿAli; in al-Tabari ʿUthman ibn Hunayf said «بعثتني ذا لحية
    وجئتك أمرد». The ʿAmmar hadith is labelled «صحيح مسلم: ويح عمار…», but that wording is al-Bukhari 447 and Muslim's is
    «تقتل عمارا الفئة الباغية» (2916). The «tawatur» rulings given to Ibn Hajar and al-Dhahabi were not found.
  - **Words from Nahj al-Balagha** appear where the histories have something else: «يا أشباه الرجال ولا رجال» (the raids
    and Sufyan's bio) and «إلا أنهم نقصوا بغيضا ونقصنا حبيبا» (Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr). Al-Tabari has «لا بل يزيد أضعافا»,
    and a different speech for the raids.
  - **The arbitration and the Khawarij are out of order.** The debate with Ibn ʿAbbas is dated after «عمرو deceived Abu
    Musa at Dumat al-Jandal», and that deception is stated as fact. In al-Bidaya the Khawarij withdrew near Kufa on the
    return from Siffin, before the arbiters met. The deception story is Abu Mikhnaf's, and Ibn al-ʿArabi calls it «كذب صراح
    ما جرى منه حرف قط». The debate event's timeline position moves to before the arbitration (D-).
  - **The mushafs:** the event follows Abu Mikhnaf (ʿAli calls it a trick, and his army forces him to accept). Ahmad's
    report from Abu Waʾil (al-Arnaʾut: «إسناده صحيح على شرط الشيخين») has ʿAli accept («نعم، أنا أولى بذلك»), with the Qurraʾ,
    later the Khawarij, wanting to fight on. The list puts Ahmad's report first and Abu Mikhnaf's after it, named as his.
  - **Invented details:** «fifty Bedouins bore false witness at al-Hawʾab… قال ابن كثير: أول شهادة زور». Al-Bidaya has no
    witnesses, and al-ʿAwasim gives the story only as the opponents' claim and answers it. Also: the «500 mushafs», ʿAli's

    the minaret», the «geostrategic» reasons for the move to Kufa, and Ibn Kathir «weakening» the burning of Muhammad ibn
    Abi Bakr (al-Bidaya relays it from Ibn Jarir without comment).
  - **Facts the sources contradict:** Dhu al-Thudayya called the Khawarij's «leader» (their emir was ʿAbd Allah ibn Wahb
    al-Rasibi). The plot is set «after the Hajj of 40 AH», but the attack was in Ramadan 40, before that Hajj. ʿAbd Allah
    ibn al-Zubayr is called ʿAʾisha's «ابن عمة»; he was her sister's son. Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr is appointed «after
    Siffin» (al-Tabari: 36 AH). ʿAbd Allah ibn Masʿada is called ʿAli's governor (he was one of Muʿawiya's commanders).
    Jarir is put over Hamadhan by ʿUmar (it was ʿUthman) and made to die «at al-Sarat in Jordan». The raids start with
    Sufyan (al-Tabari starts with al-Nuʿman). ʿAli's death is dated 21 Ramadan in two events and 19 Ramadan in a third.
- **Bios:** four bios carry sentences about the app itself (Ibn Muljam, al-Burak, ʿAmr ibn Bakr, Qatam); each is replaced

## Decisions for Mostafa

- **Abu Mikhnaf, al-Waqidi, Sayf (the session's rule: ask first).** The recommended options use none of them for a new
  fact. Where the app already says what one of them reports, the proposal names him («في رواية أبي مخنف…»). Where a

  Egypt; the Musannat in Safar 38), you choose between attributing it and dropping it. Recommended: attribute.
- **The mushafs (A- in «رفع المصاحف» and «صفين»):** Ahmad's authentic report first, with Abu Mikhnaf's after it, named
  (recommended); or Ahmad's report alone.
- **Al-ʿAwasim min al-Qawasim:** read here (Shamela 8314) and cited by page in four findings, on the Hawʾab claim, the
  arbitration and al-Daraqutni's report. Ibn al-ʿArabi's main text is kept apart from al-Khatib's notes; only the notes
  carry the isnad criticism of al-Hawʾab's Bedouin.
- **Ibn Muljam's plot (place):** «after the Hajj of 40» goes in any case. Mecca is named in none of al-Tabari, al-Bidaya,
  al-Kamil or al-Istiʿab, and Ibn Saʿd was not read. Recommended: keep the title and the Mecca map point with a note; or
  rename it «مؤامرة الخوارج الثلاثة» with no place.
- **ʿAli's day of death:** recommended, 19 Ramadan in all three events («ليلة الأحد»), with «وقيل غير ذلك»; or «in the
  last ten days of Ramadan» in all three.
- **Al-Nahrawan's year:** «38 هـ (وقيل 37 هـ)» (recommended) or keep «38 هـ».
- **Nahj al-Balagha's speech in the raids:** replace it with al-Tabari's speech, attributed to its unnamed narrator
  (recommended), or delete the speech.
- **Siyar, Minhaj al-Sunna, Ibn Saʿd, al-Masʿudi, al-Shahrastani, Ibn Khaldun, Maqatil al-Talibiyyin:** removed from the
  sources wherever the list proposes it, as in 4C–4G. Bios that end with «ترجمته في … سير أعلام النبلاء» are left as they

- **What the research changed:**
  - **Al-Hawʾab (A-009, A-010):** Ahmad's report is authentic (al-Arnaʾut: «إسناده صحيح»; Ibn Kathir: «على شرط
    الصحيحين»): «ما أظنني إلا راجعة… فقال لها الزبير: ترجعين؟ عسى الله أن يصلح بك بين الناس». It replaces the other

  - **The ʿAmmar hadith's tawatur (A-038, A-043, A-045):** Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr writes in al-Istiʿab «وتواترت الآثار عن النبي

  - **Hadith attached (his notes):** «بشّر قاتل ابن صفية بالنار» in al-Zubayr's role (Ahmad 681, al-Arnaʾut: حسن; Ibn
    Hajar: إسناده صحيح); al-Bukhari 447 named after ʿAbd Allah ibn ʿAmr's words; al-Albani's grading after Safina's hadith.
