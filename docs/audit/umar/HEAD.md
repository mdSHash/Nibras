- **171 findings on existing records:** 126 corrections (A-001 to A-126), 8 enrichment items (E-001 to E-008), 19

  Plus **6 proposed new records** (N-01 to N-06: 4 events, 2 companions), each fact with its source.
- **390 source quotes**, each found on its page by script: al-Tabari (Dar al-Turath), al-Bidaya (Dar Hajar), al-Kamil
  (Tadmuri), Usd al-Ghaba (al-ʿIlmiyya), al-Istiʿab (al-Bajawi), and Ibn Hisham (one footnote, al-Muqawqis). No event of
  this era quotes Qur'an text in its description (the `quran` references, al-Dukhan 25–28 and al-Israʾ 1, are verse
  references only, and al-Tabari confirms Saʿd recited al-Dukhan 25–28 at Kisra's iwan).
- **The worst problems:**
  - **Nine events are generic text with no source behind them** (Saqatiya, Homs, Qinnasrin, Aleppo, Antioch, al-Hadir,
    Edessa, al-Raqqa, and much of Egypt): "advanced siege tactics", "cut supply lines", "a fierce battle that lasted a
    whole day", and army sizes found in no source. Each gets a rewrite from al-Tabari, al-Bidaya or al-Kamil (A-006,
    A-036, A-065, A-075, A-079, A-083, A-090, A-097, A-101).
  - **"The battle of al-Hadir in the Jazira" (17 AH) does not exist** in the sources (A-083). Al-Hadir is Hadir
    Qinnasrin, where Khalid killed the Roman commander Minas (Sayf, al-Tabari 3/601). Proposed: rewrite it as that
    battle, moved next to Qinnasrin; alternatives in the entry.
  - **Yarmouk mixes two incompatible accounts** (A-040, A-041): it is dated Rajab 15 AH under ʿUmar (Ibn Ishaq; Ibn
    ʿAsakir: "the preserved date"), but its course of events has Abu ʿUbayda asking **Abu Bakr** for reinforcements and
    Khalid arriving from Iraq, which is Sayf's account placing Yarmouk in 13 AH. Ibn Ishaq's numbers (24,000 Muslims
    under Abu ʿUbayda; 100,000 Romans; 70,000 Roman dead) are added beside Sayf's (A-042 to A-052). Khalid's famous
    reply is misquoted and spoken to "a soldier" (a Christian Arab in al-Bidaya); Asmaʾ bint Abi Bakr's role is in no
    source read.
  - **Order of the Jazira and Sham conquests:** al-Raqqa was taken before Edessa in both accounts in al-Kamil (the app
    has the reverse and calls al-Raqqa "the last stronghold"); Aleppo and Antioch sit in 17 AH though labelled 16;
    the ʿAmwas plague sits after Edessa though Abu ʿUbayda died in it before ʿIyad marched (D-011 to D-017).
  - **Quotes reworded or misattributed:** ʿUmar's «أعزنا الله بالإسلام» (said at a ford on the way to Syria, not on
    arriving with a servant riding; A-059), Abu ʿUbayda's «أفرارًا من قدر الله» (A-094), Heraclius's farewell (A-045),
    Abu Bakr's prayer at accession not found in any listed source (A-002), al-Bara ibn Malik credited with entering
    Tustar through the water channel, when al-Tabari names Suwayd ibn al-Muthʿaba and ʿAbd Allah ibn Bishr and has al-Bara
    killed that night by al-Hurmuzan (A-104).
  - **Statements the sources contradict:** Alexandria "expelled the Byzantines from Egypt and Africa for ever" (they
    retook it under ʿUthman; A-111); Nahavand "wiped the Sasanian state off the map" (Yazdegerd lived to 31 AH; A-115);
    ʿUmar "prayed outside the Church of the Holy Sepulchre" (not in al-Tabari, al-Bidaya or al-Kamil, which have him pray
    in the mosque and clear the Rock; A-060); Dirar ibn al-Azwar among the ʿAmwas dead (A-096); al-Bidaya credited to
    al-Maqrizi in event-66's sources (A-004).
- **Bios:** nine bios carry sentences about the app itself («يذكر هنا بوصفه شخصية تاريخية…»); each is replaced by a

## Decisions for Mostafa

- **Ibn ʿAbd al-Hakam's *Futuh Misr*** (A-101, A-112): not on the reliable list, and the source of most of the Egypt and
  Alexandria details (4,000 men, a seven-month siege, the four commanders "each worth a thousand", ʿUbada ibn al-Samit,
  ʿAmr's letter about 4,000 villas and 4,000 baths, a 14-month siege, the Friday assault). Recommended: rewrite Egypt
  from al-Tabari and al-Kamil (option 1 in A-101); alternative: keep the text attributed to Ibn ʿAbd al-Hakam.
- **Ibn Saʿd's *Tabaqat*, Ibn ʿAsakir's *Tarikh Dimashq*** in four events' sources (A-005, A-064, A-089, A-125):
  keep or remove, as in 4C.
- **Al-Hadir** (A-083): rewrite as Hadir Qinnasrin (recommended), delete the event, or keep it as the Jazira campaign.
- **Homs's year** (D-005, uncertain): 15 AH (al-Tabari and al-Bidaya, Sayf) or keep 14.
- **The calendar's month** (D-007): Rabiʿ al-Awwal 16 on al-Waqidi's word, or keep "16 AH" at the year's end.
