## Summary

- **The five cities never audited with sources** (Mecca, Medina, Kairouan, Baghdad, Cordoba). 4A listed Mecca and Medina
  as «no findings» without sources; they are checked here with sources. 9 findings: Kairouan's date and founder's mandate
  (Khalifa, al-Kamil, al-Bidaya: laid out in 50 AH by order of Muʿawiya, finished 55); «أول مدينة إسلامية في المغرب» and
  «والأندلس» not found in the sources; Baghdad's «بيت الحكمة» and «العصر الذهبي» and Cordoba's «منارة العلم والحضارة في
  أوروبا» have no source in the list; enrichment with dates (Baghdad 145–146, Cordoba 92 and 138) and three sound hadiths
  for Mecca and Medina (al-Tirmidhi 3925 graded by al-Albani; al-Bukhari 1871 and 1190). Coordinates checked; all right.
  **City text is not narrated.**
- **Event 113's two steps:** «أثناء استيقاظه للصلاة أو في محرابه» → «عند خروجه لصلاة الفجر يوم الجمعة» (as the approved
  description); «بعد يومين من ضربته» → «ليلة الأحد، وقيل غير ذلك». Steps are not narrated.
- **Hujr ibn ʿAdi's bio:** al-Barzanji's verdict (page 3699): every report al-Tabari gives on Hujr's killing is Abu
  Mikhnaf's («إسناده تالف») except one with an unknown narrator. So the cause («ينكر سب علي على المنابر»), Ziyad sending
  him and Marj ʿAdhra go; what holds: Muʿawiya killed him in 51 with his companions (Khalifa), and ʿAisha's «أقتلت حجرًا؟»
  with Muʿawiya's answer (Ahmad's chain, «وهذا إسناد صحيح», page 1257). Ibn ʿUmar's grief stays (al-Istiʿab's chain
  through Ibn Abi Shayba and Nafiʿ). His role «صحابي جليل» softened to match the bio's «اختُلف في صحبته».
- **ʿAbd Allah ibn Jaʿfar:** «عن نحو 90 سنة» is al-Madaʾini's figure (Usd al-Ghaba); born in Abyssinia and ten at the
  Prophet's ﷺ death, he was about 80 in 80 AH. Proposed «عن نحو ثمانين سنة، وقال المدائني: تسعين».
- **The two authentic additions:** «قتلانا وقتلاهم في الجنة» proposed for Siffin (Musannaf, al-Hut no. 37880, page 42691;
  «سنده صحيح» in al-ʿUmayri's al-Durar article 1207, footnote 11). ʿAli's grief over Siffin (Musannaf «39007»): **not
  found** — the number is from another edition's numbering and the al-Hut Siffin chapter has no such report; the Jamal
  chapter has two regret reports, listed but not proposed.
- **The I-002 question:** one finding per approved text of ʿAli's era that still names Sayf, al-Waqidi or al-Madaʾini/ʿAwana
  (8 findings). Recommended: remove the Sayf/al-Waqidi details (Jamal's forty women, the shirt on the minbar — removed
  from two bios in 4H-2 already —, Egypt's 4,000 and «Safar»); keep the Jamal sentence you chose yourself in I-007; keep
  the raids event (wholly akhbari and says so; removing it is your decision, rule 6) and the two bio sentences that follow it.
- **25 findings** in all (11 proven, 10 likely, 4 uncertain), 36 quotes checked.
