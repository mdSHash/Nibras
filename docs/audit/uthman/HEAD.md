## Decisions for Mostafa

- **The fitna's order** (the A- entries in «قدوم وفود الأمصار» and «بداية الفتنة الكبرى»): move the march, the letter
  and the mediation to 35 AH and give 34 AH the governors' council and al-Jaraʿa (recommended), or keep them in the 34 AH
  event with the year and names corrected.
- **Al-Waqidi for the Haram expansion:** attribute the event to him (recommended) or keep it unattributed.
- **The Mushaf's year** (D-): keep 25 AH (Ibn Hajar, not on the list) or move it to 30 AH (al-Kamil).
- **Dhat al-Sawari's year** (D-): «34 هـ (وقيل 31 هـ)» (recommended) or move it to 31 AH (al-Waqidi).
- **Duplicates:** Rayy/Jurjan and Tabaristan tell the same raid of Saʿid ibn al-ʿAs; the fleet and Cyprus overlap; the
  siege and the martyrdom overlap. Recommended: keep all, each with its own part (the entries say which); merging would
  remove a visible event and needs your word.
- **Al-ʿAwasim min al-Qawasim** (Ibn al-ʿArabi), cited by four events with judgements («فرية رافضية»): not on the list and
  not read here. Recommended: replace the judgements with the sources' words (Ibn Kathir, Umm Dharr's report in al-Hakim)
  and drop the book; alternative: keep it.
- **Ibn Saʿd's Tabaqat, al-Dhahabi's Siyar, al-Azraqi, al-Samhudi, al-Dani:** keep or remove (as in 4C and 4F).
