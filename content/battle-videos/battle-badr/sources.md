# غزوة بدر — Facts check (battle-badr)

Hadith numbers follow Fuad Abd al-Baqi's numbering (Bukhari as in Fath al-Bari; Muslim, Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi as usually cited). Qur'an references are surah:ayah. Rows citing Ibn Hisham name the chapter, not a page, so they hold for any edition. All sources are Sunni. ⚠ marks a statement whose only support is weaker than a sahih hadith or the Qur'an.
